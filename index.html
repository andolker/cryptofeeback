Frequently asked questions

How do exchange referral programs work?

Exchanges pay affiliates to bring in new traders. When you open an account through someone's link, the exchange sends that person a share of every trading fee you pay, on every trade, for as long as you trade. Depending on the deal, that share is often between 20% and 50% of your fees.

Most influencers keep all of it. You pay the full fee and get nothing back.

How can you give me 20% to 40% back?

We have an affiliate agreement with OKX. Instead of keeping the whole commission, we split it with you: at least 20% of the fees you pay comes back to your account every week.

Why does the cashback go from 20% to 40%?

20% is the minimum you get back, every week. In boost weeks, announced on our Telegram, we raise it up to 40%.

Do you have access to my account or my money?

No. Your account, your funds and your keys stay with you at OKX. The cashback is paid into your OKX account. We will never ask for your password, your 2FA codes or a deposit.

When and how is the cashback paid?

Every Friday, straight to your OKX account. It covers the fees you paid during that week.

Does this change the fees I pay?

No. You pay the standard OKX fee for your tier, and 20% to 40% of it comes back to you. Your effective fee is at least 20% lower.

What does it cover? Is there a minimum?

Spot, futures and TradFi trading on OKX. There is no minimum volume and no expiration.

I already have an OKX account. Can I still get cashback?

Yes, if your account is not already linked to another referrer. Use the "Already on OKX" button to link your existing account to us. You keep your account, balance and history.

If your account is already tied to someone else's link, or the link does not work for you, message @andolker on Telegram and we will look at your options.

Why are the results approximate?

The calculator uses your 30-day volume, your order mix and the public OKX fee schedule. Your real cashback follows the fees you actually pay each week, so it moves with your trading.

Which exchanges are supported?

OKX is live today. Bybit, MEXC, Bitget, Binance and BTCC are coming soon.

Is OKX available in my country?

OKX does not serve every country. If you can open an OKX account where you live, you can get cashback.

Do you give trading signals or investment advice?

No. We only return part of your trading fees. Every trading decision is yours. Crypto involves risk; only trade what you can afford to lose.
